## [Creatine during puberty](https://github.com/Charlie-Edwards/Personal-Health/tree/main/Creatine%20during%20puberty)
Creatine is one of the most well researched supplements in existence but has large gaps in adolescent studies.

I've been taking creatine for 2 months prior to this test and have problems taking doses more than 10g due to GI levels and I always dry scoop my creatine with milk when I take it.

During this test, I'm maintaining a healthy diet, eating ~2000 calories and ~130g of protein per day and going to the gym to track my progress every time.

The pulldown weight in the `pulldown_reps` key is always 35kg, same with the bar hang, no added weight or seat and chin height.

I'm recording to see if smaller doses in younger people have any noticable changes and if it's really worth taking for a 14 year old male (me).

The creatine I'm taking is unflavoured with vitamins from Warrior ([Product link](https://teamwarrior.com/products/warrior-creatine-plus-vitamins-unflavoured)).
